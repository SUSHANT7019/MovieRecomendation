CineSet implements fundamental concepts from Discrete Mathematics

Set Operations

A ∪ B = {x | x ∈ A or x ∈ B}

Union for OR matching

Intersection

A ∩ B = {x | x ∈ A and x ∈ B}

Intersection for AND matching

Jaccard Similarity

J(A,B) = |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B|

Measures preference overlap

De Morgan's Laws

(A ∪ B)' = A' ∩ B'

Verified on every operation
